# Bypass-Website-Restrictions
Bypass Website and App Restrictions with your phone on routers with restricted website or application access by using ProtonVPN.

# Requirements
1. Any type of Android or Apple touchscreen device capable of running ProtonVPN
2. ProtonVPN from (Apple App Store)[https://apps.apple.com/se/app/proton-vpn-fast-secure/id1437005085?l=en-GB] or (Google Play Store)[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ch.protonvpn.android]
3. Android 6.0 and later or iOS/iPadOS 17.0 and later

# Disclaimer
This method is far from perfect and might not work on all devices and networks. This has been tested on 5 different devices so far and they all seem to work without any major issues. 

# iPhone and iPad
1. Download (ProtonVPN)[https://apps.apple.com/se/app/proton-vpn-fast-secure/id1437005085?l=en-GB] from the (Apple App Store)[https://apps.apple.com/se/app/proton-vpn-fast-secure/id1437005085?l=en-GB]
2. Open the ProtonVPN app when it's downloaded
3. Press "Continue as guest"
4. To bypass the restrictions, Press the purple "Connect" button on your screen
5. Allow "Proton VPN" to Add VPN Configurations
6. Type in your passcode
7. The app will automatically connect you to the best server. If it fails, press "Cancel" or "Disconnect" and try to connect again.
8. Go over to your desired app or website and start scrolling!

# Android
1. Download (ProtonVPN)[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ch.protonvpn.android] from the (Google Play Store)[https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ch.protonvpn.android]
2. Open the ProtonVPN app when it's downloaded
3. Press "Continue as guest"
4. To bypass the restrictions, Press the purple "Connect" button on your screen
5. Allow "Proton VPN" to set up a VPN connection
6. The app will automatically connect you to the best server. If it fails, press "Cancel" or "Disconnect" and try to connect again.
8. Go over to your desired app or website and start scrolling!
